# Relay from the user's sketch: f = 150 mm lens, then f = 75 mm lens (magnification 75/150 = 0.5)

Stock AC254-150-B + AC254-075-B (1 inch), two-hole stop (Ø6.25 / 9 / 12 mm, holes 13 mm apart) at lens 1, filter on the stop,
eye -> lens 1 constrained to 150-200 mm. Scripts: `src/relay_front.py`, `src/relay.py` (stop-after-lens-2 attempt); data `results/relay_front_150_75*.json`.
Same caveats as the other stock documents (glass data from memory, thickness splits assumed, filter/cover placeholders).

## Result: this layout does not work well with 1-inch stock lenses
| Case | Stop D (mm) | Eye -> lens 1 | Lens spacing (vertex gap) | m | RMS spot mean / max (um) | Rays surviving (worst field) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strict 4f: object at f1, 225 mm between lenses, sensor 75 mm behind lens 2 | 6.25 | 150 | 217 | ~0.5 | 7-25 (only 7 of 9 fields have rays) | **0%** (corner fields), 22-39% (others) |
| same | 12 | 150 | 217 | ~0.5 | 15-41 (best focus) | 3% / 31-37% |
| Optimised spacings, AC254-150 then AC254-075-B | 6.25 | 156 | 100 (lower bound) | 0.48 | **22.4 / 43.2** | 100% |
| same | 9 | 157 | 100 (lower bound) | 0.48 | 28.5 / 54.5 | 93% |
| same | 12 | 166 | 130 | 0.46 | about 35 / 56 | 78% |
| Design 3 (for comparison) | 12 | 199 | 112.5 + 79.9 | 0.53 | 9.2 / 12.6 | 92% |
| 2 x AC254-150, f/22 (for comparison) | 6.25 | 205 | 0.3 | 0.59 | 6.5 / 8.3 | 100% |

Why
* In the strict 4f layout the beams from off-axis eye points walk off the 1-inch lenses along the 225 mm afocal section (field offset 6.5 + 7.6 mm times 225/150 exceeds the 11.4 mm clear radius), so the corner fields are lost completely. Fixing that needs 2-inch lenses at both positions; the stock list only has a 2-inch f = 150 (AC508-150-B), no 2-inch f = 75.
* When the spacings are optimised the optimiser pulls the lenses together (gap hits the 100 mm bound), i.e. it abandons the 4f form, and the spots are still 2-4x larger than Design 3 because the decentered off-axis beam through a 150/75 pair is strongly aberrated.
* A hole plate after lens 2 (as drawn in the sketch) only works if it sits at the image of the stop; with the sensor 75 mm behind lens 2 that image lies beyond the sensor (112 mm behind lens 2), so the plate would have to be the stop in front of lens 1.
* Total length is about 450 mm (150 + 225 + 75), versus 262 mm for Design 3.

## Recommendation
Keep Design 3 (or the 2 x AC254-150 pair) for a stock-lens build; use the sketch relay only if you can add 2-inch optics at both positions or accept a camera-lens plus afocal-relay arrangement (not simulated).
